# 📱 Social Media Engagement Analysis Dashboard (2010–2023)

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes social media engagement data from 2010 to 2023 across major platforms—Instagram, Twitter, and Facebook—to understand user behavior, sentiment trends, hashtag effectiveness, and regional influence. The analysis aims to uncover actionable insights that can guide digital marketing strategies, content optimization, and audience targeting for improved engagement and brand loyalty.

---

## 🗂️ Data Source

This dataset contains historical engagement data from Brasco’s official social media platforms. It includes metrics such as likes, retweets, platforms, sentiment tags, hashtags, countries, and time-series patterns. The data is anonymized and aggregated to comply with privacy standards.

---

## 🛠️ Tools and Methodology

- **Microsoft Excel**: Used for data modeling, dashboard development, and interactive visuals.
- **Microsoft Excel**: Employed for data pre-cleaning, formatting, and exploratory analysis.
- **Text Analytics**: Sentiment labels were used to classify engagement types (Joy, Excitement, Neutral, etc.).

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 🧍 Sentiment-Driven Engagement
- **Joy**, **Excitement**, and **Positive** sentiment content garners the most interactions.
- Emotional resonance clearly correlates with higher engagement, indicating users connect more with upbeat content.

### 🌍 Regional Engagement Trends
- **English-speaking countries** dominate interactions, suggesting future campaigns should consider regional and cultural nuances.
- The USA alone accounts for one-third of total likes and over 4,000 retweets.

### 📱 Platform Preferences
- **Instagram** consistently outperforms other platforms in both likes and retweets.
- Twitter maintains strong retweet performance, ideal for fast-paced, viral content.

### 🧵 Hashtag Effectiveness
- Thematic hashtags, particularly those tied to emotion or visual storytelling, result in higher engagement.
- Hashtag strategies need to be emotionally intelligent and trend-aware.

---

## ✅ Recommendations

1. **Focus Content Creation Around Joy & Excitement**  
   Craft more emotionally vibrant content—especially around uplifting themes—to capitalize on the highest-performing sentiment buckets.

2. **Invest More in Instagram and Twitter**  
   Allocate more marketing resources to Instagram for likes, and Twitter for retweets, optimizing content format per platform behavior.

3. **Enhance Hashtag Strategy**  
   Use data-backed hashtags from high-performing campaigns (#Wonder, #MusicalUnity) and develop new ones tied to specific emotions or community goals.

4. **Target High-Engagement Countries with Localized Campaigns**  
   Customize content for the USA, UK, and Canada, adjusting for time zone, culture, and user behavior patterns.

5. **Revitalize Underperforming Sentiment Categories**  
   Develop creative formats around **Gratitude** and **Contentment** to better connect with passive audiences.
